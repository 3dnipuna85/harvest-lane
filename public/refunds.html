Refund Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Diamond packs are digital items delivered into the game a few seconds after payment. Orders are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, which issues every refund back to your original payment method.

When you can get a refund

  • Diamonds didn’t arrive. If your diamonds haven’t shown up within 24 hours, contact us and we’ll deliver them or refund you in full.
  • Accidental or duplicate purchase. Ask within 14 days of buying, and we’ll refund you in full as long as the diamonds from that pack haven’t been spent.
  • Charged but something went wrong. If you were charged twice or the wrong amount, we will refund the difference.

Once diamonds from a pack have been used, that pack can’t usually be refunded, because the items have already been delivered and used. We still look at every request fairly, and nothing here limits refund rights you have under your local consumer law.

When a pack is refunded, its diamonds and anything included with it may be removed from your farm.

How to ask

Email the support email on your Lemon Squeezy order receipt with your order number (it is in the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy) and what went wrong. You can also use the link in that receipt. We reply within 3 working days, and refunds usually reach your account within 5 to 10 days, depending on your bank.
