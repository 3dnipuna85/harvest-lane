Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Harvest Lane is made by cgsapiens, an individual developer in Sri Lanka. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We keep it to what the game needs.

What we collect

  • Your farm save. The state of your game (crops, coins, level and so on) is saved in your browser’s local storage. If you sign in, it is also stored in our online database so you can play on other devices.
  • Account details, if you sign in. Your email address, and if you use Google, your name and profile photo. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password.
  • Friends and leaderboard card. When signed in, a small public card with your display name, photo, level and farm snapshot is shown to friends you add. Friends can find you by your email address only if they already know it.
  • Purchases. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as Merchant of Record, which collects your name, email, billing country and payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive the order number, the pack bought and a random purchase code the game made in your browser so we can deliver the diamonds. We never receive your card details.
  • Ads. Players can choose to watch a video ad for an in-game reward. Those ads are served by Google (H5 Games Ads / AdSense), which may use cookies and device identifiers to show and measure ads, as described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
  • Technical logs. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and protect the site.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use analytics trackers. The only ads are the optional reward videos described above, which you start yourself. If this changes, we will update this policy first.

Where it is stored

Online saves and accounts are stored with Google Firebase, and purchase records with Cloudflare. Both may store data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your online save and account until you ask us to delete them. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for refunds and accounting.

Your choices

You can play as a guest without giving any personal information. You can clear your browser’s site data to delete a local save. To see, correct or delete your account and online save, email the support email on your Lemon Squeezy order receipt from the address you signed in with.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.
